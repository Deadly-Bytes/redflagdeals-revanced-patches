## Later user-authorized live downvote check

After the initial non-submitting checks, the user explicitly requested a downvote on one identified reply.

- Verified the author/body and unselected down control before tapping. Initial displayed net score was `+0`.
- First attempt showed the native "You don't have permission to vote on this post." toast, left `+0` unchanged, and left the control unselected. The stock handler produces this toast when both vote permission flags are false, before initiating a vote request.
- Refreshed the thread, found and rechecked the same reply, then retried once. The score became `-1` and the down control was selected.
- Refreshed the thread again without any further vote tap. The reply remained at `-1` with the down control selected, confirming persistence through a fresh app fetch. The successful downvote was left in place as requested.
- Source audit confirmed native net arithmetic `totalUp - totalDown + newVote - oldVote`: neutral-to-down changes the net by -1, up-to-down by -2, and undoing a downvote by +1. The extension does not maintain its own score. Zero is formatted as `+0` in this app, whereas the supplied website screenshot shows "Vote" at zero.

Live undo and error rollback remain untested. The first-attempt permission problem resolved after refresh; this check does not establish its underlying server/session/cache cause. Evidence: `vote-before.xml`, `vote-after.png` (denial toast), `vote-refreshed-target.xml`, `vote-retry.xml`, `vote-persisted.xml`, and `vote-persisted.png` in the same local evidence directory.
